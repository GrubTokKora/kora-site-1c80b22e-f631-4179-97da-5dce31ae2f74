About the event

An intimate tastings experience

Chef Dre set out to make Flavor Oasis an extraordinary culinary experience: an intimate tastings event like no other, a world of tantalizing flavors and gourmet delights, and a journey of sensory exploration.

Guests were invited to immerse themselves in a world of flavors, relish the ambiance and savor each moment of the culinary journey.

The evening offered master chef creations crafted with premium ingredients and a wide selection of flavors, with live entertainment and beverages included for a complete dining experience, and the chance to meet the culinary creator himself.
