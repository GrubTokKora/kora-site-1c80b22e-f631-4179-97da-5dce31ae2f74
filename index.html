04 Meet the chef

Andre “Dre” Christie, from Jamaica with flava.

Raised in Jamaica, Chef Dre spent his childhood in the kitchen of his grandmother, Miss Mavis. That kitchen became his first classroom: the place he learned seasoning, patience, family and care.

Two decades and a stint alongside Sandals Resorts chefs later, he leads Dre's Island Food Services, bringing that same heart to upscale private parties, weddings and galas across Florida.

“Bringing the best from us to you, the Dre's Island way.”

07 FAQ

Good to know.

Still curious? Call (772) 475-4460 or email info@dresislandfoodservices.com.

Where do you cater?

We're based in Pasco County and cater throughout Tampa Bay and Central Florida, including Wesley Chapel, Dade City, Tampa and Brandon. Further away? Just ask.

How much does catering cost?

Every event is custom-quoted based on guest count, menu and service style. Send us a few details and we'll put together a personalized quote.

How far ahead should I book?

As early as possible, especially for weddings and holiday dates. We've also pulled off last-minute requests, so it never hurts to ask.

Can you work around dietary restrictions?

Yes. We regularly create cultural and fusion dishes for specific dietary needs. Share any allergies when you request your quote.

Do you handle weddings and large events?

Absolutely. We've catered weddings and galas for up to 250 guests, plus corporate luncheons, nonprofit parties and private dinners.
